About Language App Guide

Hi, I'm Elena — the person behind Language App Guide.

I've been using language learning apps for years. Not casually — daily. I've maintained long streaks, tested premium plans, abandoned apps in frustration, and come back to them after trying the alternatives. Through all of it, I noticed something: most app reviews online are either shallow feature lists, thinly-veiled affiliate promotions, or outdated takes that don't reflect how these apps actually work today.

That's why I started this site. I wanted a place where someone could get a genuinely honest answer to questions like "Is Duolingo worth paying for?" or "Should I use Babbel or Duolingo?" — without wondering whether the reviewer was just trying to earn a commission.

What Makes This Site Different

Every review is based on real, extended use. I don't write about apps I haven't personally spent weeks or months with. When I say Duolingo's energy system is frustrating, it's because I've hit the wall myself — not because I read someone else's complaint.

No sponsorships. No affiliate deals. The apps I review haven't paid me to say anything nice about them. My recommendations are based on what I genuinely think works, not on what earns me a referral fee.

I include real user voices. Beyond my own experience, I spend a lot of time reading Reddit threads, forum posts, and community discussions to understand what real learners think. Those voices show up throughout my articles because they often say it better than I could.

I give clear recommendations. You won't find wishy-washy "it depends on your goals" conclusions here. I'll tell you who an app is best for, who should skip it, and why — so you can make an actual decision.

About Elena

I'm a language learning enthusiast who has spent years exploring different apps, methods, and resources. Language App Guide is where I share those insights so others can skip the trial-and-error phase and find the tools that actually fit their needs.
