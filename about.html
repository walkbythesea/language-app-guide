About Language App Guide

Hi, I'm Elena — the person behind Language App Guide.

I've been using language learning apps for years — not casually, but the way someone with a product manager's brain approaches a new tool: trying to understand not just whether it works, but why it was built this way. I've maintained long streaks, tested premium plans, abandoned apps in frustration, and come back to them after trying the alternatives. Through all of it, I noticed something: most app reviews online are either shallow feature lists, thinly-veiled affiliate promotions, or surface-level takes from people who don't have the background to explain what's actually going on under the hood.

That's why I started this site. I wanted a place where someone could get a genuinely honest answer to questions like "Is Duolingo worth paying for?" or "Should I use Babbel or Duolingo?" — without wondering whether the reviewer was just trying to earn a commission.

Why This Site Reads Differently

Most app reviews tell you what an app does. This site tells you why it was designed that way — and what that means for you as a learner.

I've spent 10 years as a product manager, which means I've learned to read apps the way a mechanic reads an engine. When Duolingo gives you a streak, I can explain the exact retention psychology behind that decision. When an app locks its best features behind a paywall, I can tell you whether that pricing structure is designed to help you or just to maximize conversion.

But I'm also the person who downloaded six apps in one month trying to find something that would finally stick. So I bring both lenses to every review: the analyst who understands the product decisions, and the learner who lives with the consequences.

About Elena

By day, I'm a product manager with 10 years of experience building tech products — which means I spend a lot of time thinking about how apps are designed to influence user behavior. By night (and honestly, during lunch breaks), I'm a language learner who has cycled through more apps than I care to count, looking for the ones that create real progress rather than just the feeling of it. Language App Guide is where those two obsessions collide.
