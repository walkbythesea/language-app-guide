How Much Does Rosetta Stone Cost in 2026?

By Elena | April 4, 2026

Rosetta Stone pricing is actually pretty straightforward once you know the options.

Current Rosetta Stone Prices

Plan Monthly Cost Total
Monthly (single language) $13.99/month $13.99
3-Month (single language) $11.99/month $35.97
Annual (single language) $7.99/month $95.88/year
Lifetime (all languages) One-time $299

The lifetime plan is genuinely one of the best deals in language learning if you plan to stick with it. At $299, you get permanent access to all 25+ languages — no recurring fees, ever.

Is There a Free Trial?

Yes — Rosetta Stone offers a 3-day free trial for new users. No credit card required. It gives you access to the full app including speech recognition.

Three days is enough to get a real feel for the immersive method. If you're on the fence, try it before committing to any paid plan.

Student Discounts

Rosetta Stone frequently offers 40–50% off for students. Check their official site during back-to-school season or use a .edu email for verification.

Student pricing can bring the annual plan down to around $48–$58/year — roughly what you'd pay for two months at the standard monthly rate.

Is Rosetta Stone Worth the Price?

For serious learners, yes — especially the lifetime plan. For casual learners, the monthly plan lets you try it without commitment.

Compare it against Duolingo Plus ($6.99/month annual) and Babbel ($6.95/month annual) and Rosetta Stone sits at the premium end on a per-month basis. But the lifetime deal undercuts everyone for long-term use: $299 once beats paying $84–$96/year indefinitely.

If you're deciding between these apps on price and teaching approach, the Duolingo vs Babbel vs Rosetta Stone comparison covers the full picture.

Last updated: April 4, 2026. Prices reflect standard US rates and may change.
